MASTER SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Master Subscription Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 ("Effective Date") by and between CloudTech Solutions Inc., a Delaware corporation with principal place of business at 123 Tech Park, San Francisco, CA 94105 ("Provider"), and Acme Global Corp., a New York corporation with principal place of business at 456 Corporate Plaza, New York, NY 10001 ("Customer").

1. SUBSCRIPTION SERVICES

1.1 Provision of Services. Provider shall make available to Customer the cloud-based project management software platform known as "ProjectFlow Enterprise" (the "Services") on a subscription basis. The Services shall be provided in accordance with the Service Level Agreement attached hereto as Exhibit A.

1.2 Users. Customer may authorize up to five hundred (500) individual users ("Authorized Users") to access the Services. Authorized Users shall be employees, contractors, or agents of Customer. Customer is responsible for all activities that occur under its Authorized Users' accounts.

2. FEES AND PAYMENT

2.1 Subscription Fees. Customer shall pay Provider an annual subscription fee of Two Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($250,000.00), payable in advance within thirty (30) days of invoice. Provider may increase fees upon renewal with at least fifteen (15) days prior written notice.

2.2 Late Payments. Any amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the rate of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month, or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, whichever is less. Provider reserves the right to suspend access to the Services if payment is more than ten (10) days overdue.

3. TERM AND TERMINATION

3.1 Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for a period of three (3) years (the "Initial Term"). Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one (1) year periods unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days prior to the end of the then-current term.

3.2 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice thereof. Provider may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if Customer becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

3.3 Effect of Termination. Upon termination, Customer shall immediately cease all use of the Services. Provider shall, upon Customer's written request within thirty (30) days after termination, make available to Customer an export of Customer Data in a format reasonably determined by Provider. Provider shall thereafter delete Customer Data within ninety (90) days. No refunds shall be given for unused pre-paid fees.

Provider's sole liability for breach of this warranty shall be to use commercially reasonable efforts to correct the non-conformity, or if Provider cannot correct the non-conformity, to terminate the affected Services and refund any pre-paid fees for the terminated Services.
